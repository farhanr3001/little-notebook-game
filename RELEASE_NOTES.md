Little Notebook 0.4.0 adds a fuller drawing notebook, clearer study review and more customizable rounds.

- Select several topics and question types, with balanced Mixed difficulty and history-aware question selection.
- Medium/Hard word searches include diagonals and multi-word boards, with automatic detection and found-word lines.
- Improved drawing pointers, selection/grouping, alignment guides, text, fill, custom colours, page length controls, preview and image export.
- Editable tracing imports, more outlines, customizable background paper and pink bow bindings.
- Searchable study/mistake lists, saved question layouts, mistake counts and resolve controls.
- Pet/colour editing, music removal/restoration, window modes, F11 and listening answer focus.
- Per-user installer, separate saved data, verified component updates with rollback, and complete app-data uninstall.
- Speech helpers belong to the game process and end on normal shutdown or a game crash.

Download **LittleNotebook-Setup-0.4.0.exe** below. The ZIP files are automatic-update components; they do not need to be downloaded manually. Existing installed saves are preserved by updates. Actual uninstall removes the installed game's saved data.
